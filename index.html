About InAmigos Foundation

Our Mission

InAmigos Foundation is a Central Government-licensed NGO registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act 2013, based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. Founded on September 23, 2020, we are dedicated to creating positive social change through impactful initiatives.

Our Goal: Uniting Minds for Change - we believe that collective action can transform communities and create a better future for all.
